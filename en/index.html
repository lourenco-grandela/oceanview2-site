    Location

    The sea, a 5-minute walk away.

    OceanView 2 is in Consolação, Peniche, just a 5-minute walk from the beach and 10 minutes by car from the town centre. Consolação beach is known for the qualities of its waters, traditionally associated with a high iodine content. To the north, a long stretch of sand runs towards Peniche, an area much loved by surfers and nature lovers. A place where living by the sea is part of every day.
